# King Drive BID Data Platform Decision Notes

**Q1 2027 baseline:** Use the BID's licensed CoStar snapshot, BID records, public City/County records, and field/owner verification to create the first access-controlled inventory. CoStar-derived research must remain internal unless the contract expressly permits broader use.

**Recommended ongoing research pilot:** Crexi Intelligence/All PRO is the best single published fit for a seat-based internal tool because it combines property records, ownership research, sales and lease comps, financing/market data, mapping, and governed CSV/Excel exports. It is not an outbound data API and its public terms do not establish public redistribution rights.

**Optional supplement:** REDI CRE MCP/PRO could be tested later as a second internal research source or, separately, for a CDX economic-development viewer. It requires a written review of Milwaukee coverage, machine integration, export, archival, and public-display rights.

**Source of truth:** Field survey and owner/listing-representative verification. Commercial data is a lead and research input, not a public inventory source.

**Required vendor test:** Demonstrate the same King Drive polygon and 25–30 known addresses; confirm current use, active availability, F&B classification, physical suitability fields, owner/agent data, comps, exports, Q1 snapshot retention, named users/contractors, and public-display rights.
